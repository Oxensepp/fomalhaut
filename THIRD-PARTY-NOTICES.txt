VSG (version 1.0, VGA version)
Neven Pintaric, 2000
====================================================================================================

The VSG mode of Fomalhaut is based on VSG 1.0 ("View sounds in graphics"), a
DOS program by Neven Pintaric. Six of its ten effects are re-implemented from
the program; the other effects, the colours and the palettes are additions of
Fomalhaut. The mode contains no code or data of the original program.

From the original README.TXT: "This version of VSG if [sic] Public Domain.
