Palettes (MAP files) and pictures (PCX files) distributed with Cthugha 5.3 by Torps Productions.

"Images and Colors for Cthugha" vol. 1
(C) 1989, 1993-1994 by Dmitry Altukhov (Dreamwalker / ORBiS TERTiUS), Moscow, Russia
  cryogenc.pcx  "Cryogenic Device"   (C) 1993-94 Dmitry Altukhov
  cryoheat.pcx  "Cryogenic Heat"     (C) 1994 Dmitry Altukhov
  spheres.pcx   "Spherical Travel"   (C) 1994 Dmitry Altukhov
  hunter.pcx    "The Hunter"         (C) 1989 Dmitry Altukhov
  Palettes gamma1, green, green1, heat, heat1, ice, ltgrn, sun, sun1: from FractInt, Improcess and by
  Dmitry Altukhov ("Palettes are not copyrighted, probably.")

'chromatic' color map by Todd Hedenstrom (built-in palette 2 of Cthugha).

Translation tables (TAB files) from Cthugha 5.3 and its extras (TAB.ZIP): hurr, rotate, smoke, space, spir-out,
vortex. The TAB.ZIP table generators (MKSPACE, MKSMOKE, MKHURICN, MIX_TABS) are by Ofer Faigon, Sep 1994.

Cthugha-L (Linux version 1.4)
Harald Deischinger
====================================================================================================

Includes ports of the translation table generators of Cthugha-L (tab/cmd_*.c): cmd_gentable (based on
mkgentable by Zaph), cmd_huricn, cmd_smoke and cmd_space (based on the programs by Ofer Faigon, Sep 1994),
cmd_bighalfwheel ("Krusty Wheel 1"), cmd_downspiral ("Krusty Wheel 2") and cmd_randswirls; table stretching
(stretch_trans) from Rus Maxham. The picture zaph.pcx of Cthugha-L (pcx/) is included as ZAPH-L.PCX.

Cthugha-4-Winamp (version 1.50)
Krunoslav Pisacic, 1998
====================================================================================================

The Cthugha-4-Winamp mode of Fomalhaut is a re-implementation of the Winamp visualization plug-in
Cthugha-4-Winamp 1.50 (also released for ModPlug); it includes its palette files (maps/*.map), its 3D
letters (objs/*.obj) and its picture ngc2024a_cthugha.gif (converted to PCX).
Credit from the plug-in: "Based on Cthugha for DOS & Linux ... Initial DOS version made by Kevin
Burfitt - Zaph, and a lot of others." Beat detection in the plug-in: modifications by Justin
Frankel at Nullsoft.
